## Gora — Rabindranath Tagore

**How I found it**

I had long admired Tagore's poetry and wanted to experience his longer fiction.

### What stayed with me

*Gora* explores identity, nationalism, religion, and belonging with remarkable nuance.

What impressed me most was that Tagore rarely presents easy heroes or villains. Instead, he invites the reader to question inherited beliefs.

### Common criticism

Its pace is slower than many contemporary novels, and some philosophical discussions require patience.

But the questions it raises remain deeply relevant.

## The Call of the Wild — Jack London

**How I found it**

This became the first book for a newly started book club.

Reading it alongside other people made the experience richer.

### What stayed with me

Buck's transformation from a domesticated dog into a creature rediscovering his instincts is both exciting and symbolic.
It made me think about adaptation, resilience, and the tension between civilization and nature.
The novel actually reminded me a of Fountainhead as the central idea revolves a lot around individulism and going beyong the societal convention and occasionally romanticizes survival through strength alone, overlooking some of the complexity of cooperation.
Nevertheless, it remains an incredibly compelling story.
